# Coverage of the swarm genome on real LLM agents

Generated by `node scripts/coverage.mjs` from `catalog/genome/traits.json` (39 traits, 7 axes), the flag statuses in `research/FLAGS.md`, the curated map `catalog/genome/evidence.json` and the verdicts in `results/li-verdicts.json`. Do not edit by hand; CI fails when it is stale.

Levels follow the evidence ladder in `programs/coordination.md`. **L1** here means at least one preregistered hypothesis about the trait is `supported` on real agents (the map lists the hypotheses). **L0** means a free simulation screen says the trait is worth a paid study. A flag-level test (`positive-eng`, `null-llm` in `research/FLAGS.md`) is shown as tested but earns no level, because those runs predate the preregistered LI studies. L2 and beyond (three models, a strong manager, real code, ablation) are not tracked yet: no trait has them.
