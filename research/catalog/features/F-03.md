## What the data says

About twenty words of working took one agent from 0.58 to 0.97 on the search task, where a vote of three stayed at 0.58 (V2). With the same working budget the larger model is better (1.00 against 0.87), but the small model with working costs about a twentieth per correct answer (V5). On a second task the gain of a vote did not carry over (V6).
