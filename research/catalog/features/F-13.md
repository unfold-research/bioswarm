## What the data says

Refuted (H-M01): prose and structured relay both exact in 24 of 24 through three hops (B2) and at every hop under load (B5). In the LAB-007 ring, however, the relay loses citations (38 of 144 fully cited), while one-hop aggregation is sound (24/24).

## Gaps
