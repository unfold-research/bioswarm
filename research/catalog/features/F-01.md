## What the data says

Error correlation between identical samples is 0.79; five votes add 0.01 to 0.04 (V1b). Among agents with working space a vote of three beat one agent by 0.09 (V3); among deep agents it added nothing (V7). Rewording the prompt does not help; a better member does (V4).

## Gaps

Mixing model families, the obvious way to decorrelate errors, was tried once and never as a registered test.
