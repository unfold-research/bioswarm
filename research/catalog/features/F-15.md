## What the data says

A verifier with working space brought the cascade to the level of the larger model alone (M6). Among deep agents, escalating to the larger model gave 0.87 against 0.64 for the deep agent alone (V7). Without a reliable verifier, escalating rejected answers helps but stops short of the larger model (M5).

## Gaps

Depends on a grounded verifier; specificity was 0.71 on real errors (M4).
