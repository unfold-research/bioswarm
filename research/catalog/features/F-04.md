## What the data says

With full coverage a fixed schedule is enough and undirected stigmergy loses under a silent edit (R1). With 6 reads for 12 parts and change in neighbouring parts, a local rule that follows alarms beat the best fixed schedule on two sets of 60 collections (0.72 against 0.53; 0.70 against 0.48; R2, R2b). With scattered change or a false trace it loses (R3). Agents that choose follow the signal and do not explore (D4).

## Gaps

Replicated on a synthetic task; CB2 is the first test on real repositories, where the shared work product landed below one agent.
