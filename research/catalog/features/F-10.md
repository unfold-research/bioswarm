## What the data says

An admission agent detects collisions, but its written resolution is not reliable in a small executor (I11). A regression gate in the executor works and generalises; a narrow collision is not resolved by any written card (I14). A merged card that removes an action is safe; one that must carry two actions is not followed reliably (I12).

## Gaps

Expiry and shadow mode (H-I05, H-I06) have no experiment.
