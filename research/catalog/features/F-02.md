## What the data says

A grounded verifier caught 48 of 48 planted errors with no false alarm on both models (M3). With working space it went from about 0.65 to 0.98 (M6). Choosing among five candidates beat the vote on the same five (0.87 against 0.79, M7). Behind an agent with 150 words of working it adds nothing measurable (M8).

## Gaps

On the searchers’ own real errors: specificity 0.71 and sensitivity 0.75 (M4). The verifier accepts wrong answers when it escalates (M5).
