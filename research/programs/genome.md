# Genome: testing the traits of biological swarms on AI agents

Which traits of biological swarms give a team of AI agents an advantage over the same team without the trait, and over market coordination (a manager, a shared chat), under the conditions where nature should win: **scale, latency, loss of members, open-ended work** (FINDINGS, belief 7)? Active programme since 2026-11-21 (owner); owner: Genome Research. Series: GN-S (free screens) and GN (paid studies). This file replaces the shared working plan `Shared/unfold/genoma/GN-PLAN.md` (Portuguese) as the programme of record; GN-PLAN stays as a working log.

## Boundary

- **Catalogue:** `catalog/genome/` (51 species, 39 traits on 7 axes: SIG, FLD, ACT, DEC, DOL, REG, DEF). Trait levels live in `catalog/genome/COVERAGE.md`, generated by `scripts/coverage.mjs`; this file does not repeat them.

- **Second steps of L1 traits** (SIG-05, ACT-01, DOL-04 to L2) are on the Research Leader's agenda, not this programme's.

## Funnel and rules

Same evidence ladder as [coordination](coordination.md). Each trait goes: free screen in simulation or code arms (L0) → preregistered study on real agents (L1) → a card for Colony: *adopt / test more / drop*.

- A screen's pass rule is fixed and registered (`sims/preregister.mjs`, code hash) before the first run. Default rule (from `results/sims/screen-noflag.md`): in the premise scenario the mechanism beats the **strongest** baseline by ≥ +0.05 on the primary metric (95% CI of the paired difference above 0); in the control scenario without the premise it loses no more than 0.05 (CI lower bound ≥ −0.05). Paired seeds, 400 runs per cell, equal headcount across arms (reserves, managers and sentinels count).
- No money before a preregistration on `main` approved by the Research Leader; every spend goes through the bench's single ledger. Free checks before paid ones. No tuning until it works; negative results are published as results.
- Token economy (FRENTES rule 5): each prereg states effort and why, expected cost per probe and per solved case, and whether prompts above 100k tokens are unavoidable; check whether existing LI raw data already answers the question before asking for spend.
