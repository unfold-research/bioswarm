# The track hybrid is an orchestrator with decoration

**Status: preliminary-support.**

## Result
Supported in G3b, in the model: `DOL-04` alone reaches 0.7673 against the hybrid's 0.8376, a ratio of 0.916. Removing `DOL-04` from the hybrid costs 0.4663; the six other flags are worth 0.03 together and two of them cost.

Previously: Disclosed pilot (200 test seeds, threat 0): hybrid 0.838, orchestrator 0.767, a ratio of 0.92. Ablation: removing `DOL-04` from the hybrid drops it to 0.383.

## Statement
At threat 0 on `track`, `DOL-04` alone scores at least 0.90 of the task hybrid.

## Variable
regime (`DOL-04` alone, task hybrid)

## Outcomes
track score on 200 test seeds

## Prediction
ratio of at least 0.90

## Refutation criterion
a ratio below 0.90

## Why it matters
G3a reported `track` as the one task where per-task selection still beats the best preset, and counted it as evidence that flag selection pays. If one flag reproduces 90% of it and removing that flag collapses it below the fixed schedule, the `track` result is a centralisation result and not a swarm one, and the project should say so wherever `track` is cited.
