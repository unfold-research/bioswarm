# Trace decay goes with a changing environment

**Status: open.**

## Result
Untestable in G1: every system with stigmergic deposit and a coded decay state lives in a fast or medium environment.

## Statement
Among systems that coordinate by stigmergic deposit, signal decay (FLD-02) is more frequent where the environment changes fast or at medium speed.

## Variable
environmental change fast or medium (yes/no), within FLD-01 present

## Outcomes
FLD-02 present or absent by consensus

## Prediction
higher rate where change is fast or medium, one-sided Fisher p < 0.05

## Refutation criterion
p >= 0.05, or fewer than 8 systems with FLD-01 present
