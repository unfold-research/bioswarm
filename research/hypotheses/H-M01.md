# Structured relay keeps provenance

**Status: preliminary-refuted (on the B2 and B5 instruments, 2026-11-11).** Engineering hypothesis for the Murmur programme (`programs/historical/murmur.md`).

## Statement
Findings passed between agents as structured facts with fixed source identifiers keep their citations across hops; findings passed as prose lose them.

## Variable
relay format: prose summary / structured facts with immutable source ids; hops: 1, 2, 3

## Outcomes
share of final claims whose cited source supports the exact value, claims emitted without a supporting source, decision accuracy

## Prediction
structured relay keeps exact citation support at or above 95% across three hops; prose relay drops below 70% by the second hop

## Refutation criterion
prose relay keeps citation support within 5 points of structured relay across three hops

## Strong conventional baseline
single agent reading all sources directly

## Basis in this repository
LAB-007 instrument audit (ring relay: only 38 of 144 outputs fully cited, 32 facts without supporting citation), LAB-007 aggregation audit (structured and text both 24/24 in one hop). These motivate the hypothesis; they do not test it.

## Result (B2, 2026-11-11, real model)
`results/batch2-b2-relay/REPORT.md`: prose and structured relay were both exact in 24 of 24 cases at hops 1, 2 and 3 (`claude-haiku-5-5`, 240 calls). The predicted drop of prose did not occur at this load. B5 (`results/batch2-b5-load/REPORT.md`) repeated the test with four indicators and three sources per case: again 24 of 24 for both formats at every hop.

## Evaluation scope and limits
Synthetic or licensed tasks in owned or isolated environments, at matched model budget. Cases, seeds, stopping rules and a spending cap are preregistered before any paid run.
