# A swarm solves more than one central agent past its capacity

**Status: preliminary refuted.** Registered in `tasks/LI1-LATERAL-INHIBITION-PREREG.md`.

## Result
LI1: nobody solved a 240-node case. Every symmetry-breaking arm ended below the central agent in 12 of 12, but that agent returned nothing in 22 of 96 calls and was anchored on the swarm start. Independent review: `docs/reviews/LI-REVIEW-2026-11-17.md`.

## Statement
At 240 nodes the better of inhibition and recruitment solves more cases than a central agent.
