# Recruit toward verified leads

**Status: open.** Proposed engineering hypothesis for the Murmur programme (`programs/historical/murmur.md`). No experiment has run.

## Statement
Giving more search budget to leads that already produced verified sources finds more supported claims per dollar than splitting the budget evenly.

## Variable
budget allocation: uniform over leads / proportional to verified sources per lead, with an exploration floor / winner-takes-all

## Outcomes
supported claims per dollar, coverage of sub-questions, share of budget spent on dead leads

## Prediction
proportional allocation with a floor beats uniform on supported claims per dollar; winner-takes-all loses coverage

## Refutation criterion
uniform allocation matches proportional allocation, or the floor is not needed to keep coverage

## Strong conventional baseline
fixed plan written by a lead agent before any search

## Basis in this repository
sim-quorum (strong recruitment herds and decides wrong), LAB-006 allocator (wins in some regimes only), H-20. These motivate the hypothesis; they do not test it.

## Evaluation scope and limits
Synthetic or licensed tasks in owned or isolated environments, at matched model budget. Cases, seeds, stopping rules and a spending cap are preregistered before any paid run.
