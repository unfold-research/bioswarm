# A leaderless regime beats the orchestrator under threat

**Status: preliminary-support-degenerate.**

## Result
Satisfied as written (schedule on maintain +0.109, independent members on pipeline +0.107, both separable) but on degenerate cases: the fixed schedule communicates nothing and "independent" has no flags at all. No leaderless collective mechanism beats a centralized one anywhere in this instrument. Registered defect; see `results/genome-g3b/REPORT.md`.

Previously: registered on 2026-11-15 before the test seeds were read for this test.

## Statement
At threat 0.2, at least one `DOL-04`-free regime beats the orchestrator on at least one of the four tasks.

## Variable
regime (any preset or task hybrid without `DOL-04`, against `DOL-04` alone)

## Outcomes
task score on 200 test seeds at threat 0.2

## Prediction
at least one task where a leaderless regime wins

## Refutation criterion
no leaderless regime beats the orchestrator on any of the four tasks

## Why it matters
G2 showed reading others is the orchestrator's vulnerability (H-G11: it loses 0.34 under threat on track). G3a and G3b show centralisation explains most of what this instrument rewards. Whether a swarm without a leader wins anywhere is the question the whole project rests on, and no run has yet asked it directly. A failure here means the next paid study should not be built on that premise.
