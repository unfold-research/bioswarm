# Shadow mode before action

**Status: open.** Proposed engineering hypothesis of the instinct family (`programs/historical/instincts.md`). No experiment has run.

## Statement
Running a newly received instinct in shadow, where it reports what it would do without acting, catches harmful instincts at a cost well below the damage they would cause.

## Variable
admission policy: act immediately / shadow for k decisions then promote if agreement with accepted outcomes exceeds a bar

## Outcomes
harmful firings that reach action, useful firings delayed, extra tokens

## Prediction
shadow admission blocks at least 90% of planted harmful instincts while delaying useful ones by k decisions

## Refutation criterion
harmful instincts pass shadow as often as useful ones

## Strong conventional baseline
human approval of every new rule

## Basis in this repository
H-D10 (memory quarantine and verified reuse), H-D13. These motivate the hypothesis; they do not test it.

## Evaluation scope and limits
Synthetic tasks in owned or isolated environments, at matched model budget. Cases, seeds, stopping rules and a spending cap are preregistered before any paid run.
