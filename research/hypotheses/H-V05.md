# Bound the fan-out

**Status: open.** Proposed engineering hypothesis for the Void programme (`programs/historical/void.md`). No experiment has run.

## Statement
A coding swarm stays stable only while each task spawns fewer than one surviving subtask on average; a spawn budget with cancellation keeps it there.

## Variable
spawn policy: unbounded / depth limit / cancellation of orphaned and duplicate subtasks; measured children per task and cancelled fraction

## Outcomes
subtasks per root task, backlog after the root is accepted, cost per accepted task

## Prediction
runs whose measured effective children per task is 1 or more show a backlog that keeps growing after acceptance; cancellation of orphans brings it below 1 at lower cost than a depth limit

## Refutation criterion
backlog stays bounded with measured effective children per task above 1, or a depth limit is cheaper than cancellation

## Strong conventional baseline
hard cap on concurrent agents

## Basis in this repository
sim-branching (in the model), H-24, H-14, H-21. These motivate the hypothesis; they do not test it.

## Evaluation scope and limits
Synthetic or licensed tasks in owned or isolated environments, at matched model budget. Cases, seeds, stopping rules and a spending cap are preregistered before any paid run.
