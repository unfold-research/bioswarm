# An instinct replaces deliberation at lower cost

**Status: testing (Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5, 2026-11-11).** Proposed engineering hypothesis of the instinct family (`programs/historical/instincts.md`). No experiment has run.

## Statement
An explicit trigger-and-action rule gives an agent the same decision quality as asking it to reason about the situation, with fewer output tokens.

## Variable
how the behaviour is obtained: no guidance / explicit instinct / instruction to deliberate about the same situation

## Outcomes
correct decisions where the instinct should fire, wrong firings where it should not, output tokens per decision

## Prediction
the instinct matches or beats deliberation on correct decisions and wrong firings, and uses fewer output tokens

## Refutation criterion
deliberation is at least two cases in eight better on correct decisions, or the instinct fires wrongly more often

## Strong conventional baseline
the same model with a general instruction to reason carefully

## Basis in this repository
sim-response-threshold (allocation without messages), H-16 (triage does not pay for orchestration), B2 (the instruction, not the format, kept provenance). These motivate the hypothesis; they do not test it.

## Result (2026-11-11, Claude Sonnet 5.5)
`results/batch2-r1-sonnet/REPORT.md`: deliberation matched the instinct on decisions (8 of 8, no wrong firing in all three families), so the preregistered test that deliberation would be worse was not supported. The instinct used about 200 output tokens per decision against about 310.

## Result (I1, 2026-11-11, Claude Haiku 5.5)
`results/batch2-i1-transplant/REPORT.md`: deliberation fired in 7, 6 and 8 of 8 against 5, 8 and 8 for the instinct, at about 320 output tokens against 200. On both models the decisions are close and the instinct is cheaper; the preregistered test that deliberation would be worse was not supported.

## Evaluation scope and limits
Synthetic tasks in owned or isolated environments, at matched model budget. Cases, seeds, stopping rules and a spending cap are preregistered before any paid run.
