# Lateral inhibition beats simultaneous repair

**Status: preliminary refuted.** Registered in `tasks/LI1-LATERAL-INHIBITION-PREREG.md`.

## Result
LI1: neither arm solved any of 36 cases (refuted on the registered endpoint); on final conflicts inhibition was lower in 36 of 36 paired cases (sign p = 3e-11), and 24 of 24 in LI3. Independent review: `docs/reviews/LI-REVIEW-2026-11-17.md`.

## Statement
In parallel LLM repair of a coupled task, giving each cross-owner conflict to one side solves more cases than letting every touched member act.
