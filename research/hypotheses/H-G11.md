# Reading others costs under threat

**Status: preliminary-support.**

## Result
Supported in G2, in the model: with 20% compromised members, claim-reading regimes lose 0.16 on average against 0.04 for independent members.

## Statement
With 20% compromised members, regimes that read others' claims lose at least 0.05 more than independent members.

## Variable
threat 0 or 0.2; regime

## Outcomes
score loss

## Prediction
claim-reading loss exceeds independent loss by >= 0.05

## Refutation criterion
difference below 0.05
