# Verify claims before adding searchers

**Status: preliminary-support (Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5, 2026-11-11; the comparison with adding searchers has not run).** Proposed engineering hypothesis for the Murmur programme (`programs/historical/murmur.md`). No experiment has run.

## Statement
An independent check of each claim against its cited source raises the precision of a research answer more than more searchers do.

## Variable
extra budget spent on: more searchers / an independent claim-to-source verifier / both

## Outcomes
precision of supported claims (human-checked sample), recall of known facts, cost per supported claim

## Prediction
the verifier raises precision more than doubling the searchers at equal budget; precision is capped by the verifier specificity

## Refutation criterion
doubling the searchers raises precision as much as the verifier at equal budget

## Strong conventional baseline
self-check by the same agent that wrote the claim

## Basis in this repository
sim-verifier, library evidence table (few studies compare at matched compute), H-02, H-13. These motivate the hypothesis; they do not test it.

## Result (2026-11-11, Claude Sonnet 5.5)
`results/batch2-r1-sonnet/REPORT.md`: a verifier with access to the source records caught 48 of 48 planted provenance errors with 48 of 48 clean reports passed; without the sources it caught 1 of 48. No comparison with adding searchers yet.

## Result (M3, 2026-11-11, Claude Haiku 5.5)
`results/batch2-m3-verifier/REPORT.md`: grounded verifier 48 of 48 corrupted reports flagged and 48 of 48 faithful reports passed; blind verifier 11 of 48 and 45 of 48. Same pattern as on Sonnet 5.5.

## Evaluation scope and limits
Synthetic or licensed tasks in owned or isolated environments, at matched model budget. Cases, seeds, stopping rules and a spending cap are preregistered before any paid run.

## Result (M4, Claude Haiku 5.5, 2026-11-11)
`results/batch2-m4-budget/REPORT.md`: at three and at five calls, verifying and falling back on another sample gave the same accuracy as voting and as a single call (difference 0), because five samples of one prompt returned one answer in 82 of 90 tasks. The verifier rejected 0.75 of the searchers' own errors and accepted 0.71 of their correct answers; answers it accepted were right 0.69 of the time against 0.46 unfiltered. The comparison half of the hypothesis is not supported.

## Result (M5, Claude Haiku 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5, 2026-11-11)
`results/batch2-m5-second/REPORT.md`: with the verifier of M4, a reworded fallback gave no gain (−0.05), a fallback told of the rejection was worse (−0.13, interval below zero) and a vote over three wordings gave none. Escalating rejected answers to Sonnet raised accuracy from 0.68 to 0.83, against 1.00 for Sonnet alone. Verifier specificity 0.67, sensitivity 0.70.

## Result (M6 and V3, Claude Haiku 5.5, 2026-11-11)
`results/batch2-v3-collective/REPORT.md`: among agents with working space, verify3 beat a vote of three at the same number of calls (0.82 against 0.76, interval of the difference 0.02 to 0.12). First support for the hypothesis as stated. `results/batch2-m6-verifier/REPORT.md`: on an answer-only searcher, one call with working space equalled the whole cascade.

## Result (M7, Claude Haiku 5.5, 2026-11-11)
`results/batch2-m7-select/REPORT.md`: with five reasoning candidates, selection by a verifier reached 0.87 against 0.79 for the vote (interval of the difference 0.01 to 0.14). One agent with 150 words of working reached 0.91 in one call; not separable from the selected collective.

## Result (M8, Claude Haiku 5.5, 2026-11-11)
`results/batch2-m8-deep/REPORT.md`: behind a deep agent (0.94 alone) a verifier with fallback added 0.01 to 0.02, interval touching zero; it caught 2 or 3 of the 5 errors. The support from V3 and M7 holds for shallow searchers only.

## Result (V6, Claude Haiku 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5, 2026-11-11)
`results/batch2-v6-second/REPORT.md`: on a second task verify3 beat vote3 again (0.70 against 0.64, interval of the difference 0.01 to 0.11); the verifier accepted 58 of 62 correct answers and rejected 27 of 28 wrong ones.

## Result (V7, Claude Haiku 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5, 2026-11-11)
`results/batch2-v7-hard/REPORT.md`: with the deep agent at 0.64, verified selection among three (0.67) and a vote (0.63) added nothing; the same-model verifier fell to 0.72 and 0.81. Escalating rejected answers to Sonnet gave 0.87. Not supported among deep agents of one model.
