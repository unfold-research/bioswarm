# Unseen collectives resemble social insects

**Status: preliminary-support.**

## Result
Supported in G1: mean Jaccard of unseen collectives with social insects 0.27, with vertebrates 0.14 (permutation p < 0.001).

## Statement
Microbial and cellular collectives are closer in trait profile to the social insects than to vertebrate groups.

## Variable
taxon block of the comparison (insects, vertebrates)

## Outcomes
mean Jaccard similarity of present-trait sets

## Prediction
unseen x insects above unseen x vertebrates, permutation p < 0.05

## Refutation criterion
difference <= 0 or permutation p >= 0.05
