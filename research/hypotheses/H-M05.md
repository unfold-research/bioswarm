# Stop on independent agreement, reopen on a counterexample

**Status: open.** Proposed engineering hypothesis for the Murmur programme (`programs/historical/murmur.md`). No experiment has run.

## Statement
Stopping when a set number of independent sources agree is cheaper than a fixed search depth, provided one verified counterexample reopens the question.

## Variable
stopping rule: fixed depth / stop at Q independent agreeing sources / the same plus reopening on a verified contradicting source

## Outcomes
answer accuracy on questions with a known answer, sources read per question, unanimous errors

## Prediction
stopping at Q = 3 independent sources reads at least 30% fewer sources than fixed depth at equal accuracy; without the reopening rule unanimous errors rise when sources share an origin

## Refutation criterion
fixed depth is as cheap at equal accuracy, or reopening does not reduce unanimous errors

## Strong conventional baseline
fixed depth with a final single-agent synthesis

## Basis in this repository
sim-quorum (not supported in the model: gain was convex and bias-sensitive), H-08, H-12, H-27. These motivate the hypothesis; they do not test it.

## Evaluation scope and limits
Synthetic or licensed tasks in owned or isolated environments, at matched model budget. Cases, seeds, stopping rules and a spending cap are preregistered before any paid run.
