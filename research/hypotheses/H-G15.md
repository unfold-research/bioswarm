# The informed channel was carrying the school archetype

**Status: preliminary-support.**

## Result
Supported in G3a, in the model (2 of 4 tasks, exactly at threshold; see the paired table in `results/genome-g3a/REPORT.md`). `maintain` and `mass` shrink past half; `track` does not move because it uses the separate perception edge; `pipeline` was already negative and had nothing to shrink.

## Statement
Under a `DEC-04` that pays for its observation channel, the school archetype's advantage over independent members shrinks by at least half on at least 2 of the 4 main tasks at threat 0.

## Variable
engine version (G2 uncorrected, G3a corrected); archetype (school, independent)

## Outcomes
task score on the 200 test seeds, per task, paired by seed

## Prediction
shrinkage of at least half on 2 of 4 tasks

## Refutation criterion
fewer than 2 of 4

## Why it matters
`DEC-04` was free perfect sensing over 17 sites in the uncorrected engine (`observatory/genome/sim/engine.mjs`, the `a.informed` line in `decide`), which the G2 report flagged in its note 6. If the correction does not move the school archetype, the note was wrong about the cause. If it does, every G2 cell where `DEC-04` entered is superseded.
