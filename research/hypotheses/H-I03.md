# A card is enough

**Status: open.** Proposed engineering hypothesis of the instinct family (`programs/historical/instincts.md`). No experiment has run.

## Statement
A compact card (trigger, action, exceptions) transfers as well as a long explanation with worked examples.

## Variable
form of the transplanted instinct: compact card / card plus rationale / card plus worked examples

## Outcomes
effect in the recipient, wrong firings, input tokens added per call

## Prediction
the compact card is within one case in eight of the longer forms

## Refutation criterion
worked examples add two or more cases in eight of effect

## Strong conventional baseline
few-shot examples without a stated rule

## Basis in this repository
B2 (a one-line instruction about what must survive was sufficient). These motivate the hypothesis; they do not test it.

## Evaluation scope and limits
Synthetic tasks in owned or isolated environments, at matched model budget. Cases, seeds, stopping rules and a spending cap are preregistered before any paid run.
