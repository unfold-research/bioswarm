# The informed perception edge is not load-bearing on track

**Status: preliminary-refuted.**

## Result
Refuted in G3b, in the model: the edge at 0.9 against 0.65 gives 0.8376 against 0.7962, a paired difference of 0.0414 ± 0.0074 over 200 test seeds. The disclosed 100-seed pilot that suggested no effect was wrong. G3a's attribution was accidentally right about the direction and wrong about the magnitude.

Previously: Disclosed pilot (100 test seeds, threat 0): 0.836 with the edge at 0.9, 0.836 with it at 0.65.

## Statement
At threat 0 on `track`, the task hybrid scores within 0.02 of its own score when the informed direction edge is set to 0.65.

## Variable
informed direction accuracy (0.9 against 0.65)

## Outcomes
track score on 200 test seeds, paired by seed

## Prediction
difference below 0.02

## Refutation criterion
a difference of 0.02 or more

## Why it matters
G3a closed by attributing the survival of the `track` hybrid to this free edge. The pilot contradicts that. If the registered run confirms it, G3a's closing reading is wrong and its report needs a correction notice, and `track` stops counting as evidence for distributed coordination.
