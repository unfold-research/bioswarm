# Recruit on a failing check, not by default

**Status: open.** Proposed engineering hypothesis for the Void programme (`programs/historical/void.md`). No experiment has run.

## Statement
Calling a helper only when a verifiable check fails uses far fewer agents than a fixed team at the same acceptance.

## Variable
recruitment rule: fixed team of 3 / helper when visible tests fail / helper when agents disagree

## Outcomes
agents per task, cost per accepted task, acceptance rate

## Prediction
recruitment on a failing check uses at least 40% fewer agent-runs than the fixed team at equal acceptance; disagreement is a weaker trigger than a failing check

## Refutation criterion
the fixed team has equal or lower cost per accepted task, or disagreement triggers as well as failing checks

## Strong conventional baseline
fixed team of 3 and single agent with the same total budget

## Basis in this repository
sim-response-threshold, sim-clonal, H-06, H-18. These motivate the hypothesis; they do not test it.

## Evaluation scope and limits
Synthetic or licensed tasks in owned or isolated environments, at matched model budget. Cases, seeds, stopping rules and a spending cap are preregistered before any paid run.
