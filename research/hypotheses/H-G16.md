# The chemical mass hybrid survives the cost

**Status: preliminary-support-erratum.**

## Result
Satisfied trivially: the `mass` hybrid at threat 0.2 never contained `DEC-04`, so the correction could not touch it. Margin 0.345 against the orchestrator, unchanged from G2. The test did not test what it claimed; see the erratum in `results/genome-g3a/REPORT.md`.

## Statement
Under a corrected `DEC-04`, the `mass` task hybrid at threat 0.2 still beats the orchestrator by at least 0.10.

## Variable
regime (task hybrid for mass, orchestrator)

## Outcomes
mass-task score on 200 test seeds at threat 0.2

## Prediction
margin of at least 0.10

## Refutation criterion
a margin below 0.10, or not separable from zero

## Why it matters
This is G2's strongest positive claim and the cell where the unearned channel was most load-bearing: hybrid 0.45 against orchestrator 0.11, and the hybrid's winning flag is `DEC-04`. If the margin survives the correction, the hybrid is a real finding. If it collapses, G2's `mass` result was an artifact of free information.
