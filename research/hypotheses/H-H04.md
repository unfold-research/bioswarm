# An acceptance gate in the loop cuts false success

**Status: refuted in one study.** Hypothesis of the Harness division (`programs/harness.md`). Study 3 (`results/harness-study-3-2026-11/REPORT.md`): the gate fired in 10 of 10 tasks and the team wrote and passed its own tests, yet it accepted no more tasks than the arm without the gate (8 and 8) and had the same false successes, at 131% of the cost. Every false success was one hidden test that the team's own tests, written from the same reading of the specification, could not see.

## Statement
An acceptance gate enforced in the lead's loop lowers false success on coupled tasks without a large rise in cost. The lead may not finish until every package has the team's own spec tests and they pass with the visible ones.

## Variable
acceptance before done: none / gate in the lead's loop (the Unfold extension's `gate`)

## Outcomes
false success, accepted tasks, cost per accepted task, gate firings

## Prediction
false success at most one in ten coupled tasks, at least two more tasks accepted than without the gate, cost per accepted task at most 130% of the arm without it

## Refutation criterion
no more tasks accepted than without the gate, or no fewer false successes
