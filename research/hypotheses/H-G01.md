# The swarm-genome coding instrument is usable

**Status: preliminary-support.**

## Result
Supported in G1 (`results/genome-g1/REPORT.md`): κ 0.69 between coders; 95.2% of cited DOIs resolve to the cited work.

## Statement
Two independent model coders agree on trait states with pooled Cohen's kappa of at least 0.6, and at least 80% of the references they cite with a DOI resolve to the cited work.

## Variable
coder (Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5); citation source

## Outcomes
kappa over collapsed states; share of verified DOIs

## Prediction
kappa >= 0.6 and verified share >= 0.8

## Refutation criterion
kappa < 0.6 or verified share < 0.8
