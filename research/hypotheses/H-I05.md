# An instinct needs an expiry

**Status: open.** Proposed engineering hypothesis of the instinct family (`programs/historical/instincts.md`). No experiment has run.

## Statement
An instinct that is never re-validated keeps firing after the situation that justified it has changed; an expiry tied to reinforcement removes it in bounded time.

## Variable
retention rule: permanent / expires unless reinforced by accepted outcomes; rate of change of the environment

## Outcomes
wrong firings after a change, time to stop firing, effect lost while the instinct is still valid

## Prediction
a permanent instinct keeps firing wrongly for a time proportional to its history; an expiring one stops in a time set by the expiry

## Refutation criterion
permanent instincts stop firing wrongly as fast as expiring ones

## Strong conventional baseline
manual review of the rule set on a schedule

## Basis in this repository
sim-router-decay (522 tasks to readapt without decay against 34 with it), H-25, H-07. These motivate the hypothesis; they do not test it.

## Evaluation scope and limits
Synthetic tasks in owned or isolated environments, at matched model budget. Cases, seeds, stopping rules and a spending cap are preregistered before any paid run.
