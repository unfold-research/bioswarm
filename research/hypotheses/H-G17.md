# Flag selection still pays per task after the correction

**Status: preliminary-refuted.**

## Result
Refuted in G3a, in the model: 1 of 4 tasks at threat 0 (needed 2); 2 of 4 at threat 0.2. Per-task selection now pays only on `track` at both levels and on `mass` under threat.

## Statement
Under a corrected `DEC-04`, the task hybrid beats the best preset by at least 0.03 on at least 2 of 4 tasks at each threat level.

## Variable
regime (task hybrid, best preset)

## Outcomes
task score on 200 test seeds, per task and threat

## Prediction
2 of 4 tasks at both threat 0 and threat 0.2

## Refutation criterion
fewer than 2 of 4 at either threat level

## Why it matters
This re-tests H-G13 (supported on the uncorrected engine) on the corrected one, so it is the test of whether per-task selection was itself an artifact. Same prediction, same seeds, so a change of verdict is attributable to the treatment.
