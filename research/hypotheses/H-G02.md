# Quorum decisions belong to site choice

**Status: open.**

## Result
Not tested in G1 for lack of power: quorum decision was never coded absent, so both groups were all present (2 of 2 and 5 of 5). Registered verdict: refuted; it is not evidence against the idea.

## Statement
Quorum decision (trait DEC-01) is more frequent in collective systems whose problems include choosing a site.

## Variable
main collective problems include site-choice (yes/no)

## Outcomes
DEC-01 present or absent by consensus

## Prediction
higher rate in the site-choice group, one-sided Fisher p < 0.05

## Refutation criterion
p >= 0.05 or the difference has the wrong sign
