# Stimulus-centred recruitment is cheaper than fixed territories

**Status: preliminary support.** Registered in `tasks/LI1-LATERAL-INHIBITION-PREREG.md`.

## Result
LI1: 2 against 0 solved, 59k against 168k output tokens per case. Review: `patch` averaged 0.98 calls per round (one agent on a 40-node window), so the support is for a windowed single agent, not for recruitment of many members. Independent review: `docs/reviews/LI-REVIEW-2026-11-17.md`.

## Statement
Recruiting the neighbourhood of each conflict solves at least as many cases as inhibition at fewer tokens.
