# Still no general hybrid after the correction

**Status: preliminary-support.**

## Result
Supported in G3a, in the model: the general hybrid (`DEC-04, DEF-01, DOL-04`) is within 0.05 of the best regime in 3 of 8 cells, against a threshold of at most 5.

## Statement
Under a corrected `DEC-04`, the general hybrid is within 0.05 of the best regime of each cell in at most 5 of 8 cells.

## Variable
regime (general hybrid, best regime per cell)

## Outcomes
task score on 200 test seeds, per cell

## Prediction
at most 5 of 8 cells

## Refutation criterion
6 or more of 8

## Why it matters
This re-tests H-G14 (refuted on the uncorrected engine) with the same 6-of-8 threshold. A reversal would mean the correction creates a general hybrid where the free channel had blocked one; support at 5 or fewer means per-task selection stays the rule.
