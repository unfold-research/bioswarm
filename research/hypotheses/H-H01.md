# Rules in the loop beat the same rules as advice

**Status: refuted in one study.** Hypothesis of the Harness division (`programs/harness.md`). Refuted in the first study (`results/harness-study-2026-11/REPORT.md`): with a pair on well-specified repair tasks, the in-loop rules cut agent messages by 38% but left cost per accepted task level (103% of the arm without rules). Coordination was a small share of cost there. The hypothesis stays open for tasks where coordination drifts. Second study (`results/harness-study-2-2026-11/REPORT.md`, trios on coupled tasks): inconclusive. U cost 96% of P per accepted task, at equal acceptance, with 38% fewer messages and messages fixed at 4 per task against 4 to 12.

## Statement
The same model on the same tasks costs less per accepted task when the coordination rules run in the agent's loop than when they are given as instructions.

## Variable
where the rules live: none / instructions in the agent's prompt / enforced in the loop (Pi extension)

## Outcomes
cost per accepted task, rejections, agent messages, rule firings

## Prediction
in the loop: at most 70% of the cost per accepted task of the arm without rules, with no more than one extra rejection in 10 to 15 pairs; instructions: between the two

## Refutation criterion
in-loop rules cost as much or more per accepted task than instructions or no rules, or raise rejections by two or more
