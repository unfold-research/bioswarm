# Castes and reserve come with size

**Status: partial support.**

## Result
Partly supported in G1: castes follow size (20 of 27 against 2 of 15, p < 0.001; one-per-taxon p̃ 0.12); inactive reserve had no absent cell. Refuted as registered because both were required.

## Statement
Morphological castes (DOL-01) and an inactive reserve (ACT-04) are more frequent in groups of at least 1,000 members.

## Variable
typical group size >= 1,000 (yes/no)

## Outcomes
DOL-01 and ACT-04 present or absent by consensus

## Prediction
both traits at a higher rate in large groups, each one-sided Fisher p < 0.05

## Refutation criterion
either p >= 0.05
