# A spending ceiling in the loop cuts the tail

**Status: open.** Hypothesis of the Harness division (`programs/harness.md`). No study has run.

## Statement
A per-request spending ceiling enforced in the loop removes the costliest requests without raising rejections.

## Variable
per-request ceiling: none / [US$ redacted] / [US$ redacted]

## Outcomes
cost distribution (p90, max), rejections, answers that say what is left undone

## Prediction
the tail above the ceiling disappears; rejections rise by at most one in 10 to 15 pairs; stopped answers state what is left

## Refutation criterion
rejections rise by two or more, or stopped answers hide unfinished work
