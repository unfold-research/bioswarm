# Leaders belong to small groups of capable members

**Status: preliminary-support.**

## Result
Supported in G1: 8 of 9 small groups of capable members have a leader or hierarchy against 2 of 30 others (p < 0.001); weaker with one system per taxon group (p̃ 0.09).

## Statement
A persistent leader or dominance hierarchy (DOL-04) is more frequent in groups of under 100 members of medium or high individual cognition.

## Variable
small group of capable members (yes/no)

## Outcomes
DOL-04 present or absent by consensus

## Prediction
higher rate in that group, one-sided Fisher p < 0.05

## Refutation criterion
p >= 0.05 or the difference has the wrong sign
