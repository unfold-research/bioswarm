# G3a's result does not depend on the survey cap

**Status: preliminary-refuted.**

## Result
Refuted in G3b, in the model: 7 of 8 cells are invariant to four decimals across the survey cap, but `pipeline`/school ranges 0.0224 (cap 0: 0.5666, cap 2: 0.5451, flat from 2 to 8), above the 0.02 threshold. The declared cap of 4 is not doing hidden work; the presence of the survey is.

Previously: registered on 2026-11-15; closes the limitation G3a listed as untested.

## Statement
School and the task hybrids change by less than 0.02 across `informedSense` of 0, 2, 4 and 8, on all four tasks at threat 0.

## Variable
survey cap (`informedSense`: 0, 2, 4, 8)

## Outcomes
task score on 50 sensitivity seeds (3000–3049), per task, per regime

## Prediction
range below 0.02 in every cell

## Refutation criterion
a range of 0.02 or more in any cell

## Why it matters
G3a declared `informedSense = 4` without tuning and listed the lack of a sensitivity check as a limit. If the result is cap-invariant, the declared constant is not doing hidden work and the G3a findings stand as reported. If it is not, the G3a numbers are an artifact of one arbitrary choice and the study needs re-running.
