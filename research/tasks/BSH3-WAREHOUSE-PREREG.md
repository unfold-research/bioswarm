# BSH3 · return a missing capacity to its owner

Four new synthetic Brazilian allocations (seven whole orders each), three arms on each instance. The first two are recovery stress tests: deliver an identical partial capacity record with C removed (Agreste), or an empty record (Sertão), to every arm. The other two use the unmodified first warehouse reply (Mata, Litoral). The original raw message is retained even when replaced by a fixture. Measure controlled and natural cases separately. New freight matrices and capacities are frozen in bench/bioswarm/warehouse.mjs before paid calls. No historical answers or oracle values enter model context.

Match shared commercial and warehouse messages, model (gpt-6-luna flex), tariffs, validator and two proposal opportunities. Shared specialists each run once per case. Their costs count once in actual spend and once per arm in full per-configuration cost estimates. Vary arm order by case. Manager sees the records, advises transport, and has the usual transport repair; current Unfold routes records via stigwait with the same repair. Augmented Unfold inspects N,C,S on the common numeric-map interface. For any missing or nonnegative-integer-invalid field it calls the warehouse once with the specific missing IDs and that specialist's own source. Merge numeric fields, then route and plan normally. No numbers are supplied by the validator. A present but wrong integer is outside this intervention. Distinguish verified source accuracy of returned values from merely populated fields.

Primary endpoints: source-exact restoration and feasible final plan under each forced omission. Also report first-proposal success, exact gap to exhaustive optimum, unforced omissions and false/unnecessary returns, and resource use. Measure the actual return-call price and elapsed time, plus each arm's total gateway cost including its common preparation and its incremental planning latency after the shared notes. An unsolved case incurs its cost and is reported as a failure. A correct guess without the record counts as a valid allocation but not evidence of restored communication. Four cases are descriptive, not a population estimate. All roles use JSON, including an explicit flat N,C,S interface for first warehouse replies across arms.
