# BSH8 · Bioinspired discovery cue

One unseen constrained control problem, two isolated simulated teams of three sequential model roles: designer, reviewer, finisher. Identical gpt-6-luna flex, no tools, 2048 completion tokens per role, three submissions, same 12-minute deadline from concurrent start, [US$ redacted] reserve and six transport attempts per team. Both get the same public engineering task, features, baseline and development feedback on seeds 1001–1009. Random anonymous team labels and treatment assignment before calls. Only treatment receives a response-threshold/social-insect cue (local stimuli, different sensitivities and exploration); control receives the ordinary engineering brief. No previous Unfold results in context. Equal constrained search space, not open-ended invention. Each submission is six bounded real weights. Retain earlier best development policy at end; ties earliest. Hidden seeds randomly drawn before calls, 30 per regime, seed hash sealed; simulator exogenous randomness shared across arms and independent of action. Anonymous deterministic grader receives only numerical policies, labels and seeds, never prompts, rationale or assignment. Freeze scores before unblinding. Primary endpoint total residual drift relative to age-only baseline on all 90 cases (lower is better). Secondary: first timestamp and role reaching >=5% aggregate hidden improvement; timing measured at response delivery, includes inference latency. Report each checkpoint, mean paired loss by regime, costs, deadline and errors, no winner if tie. Single task, one team per treatment, no population or causal generalization.
